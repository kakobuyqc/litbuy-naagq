The quick answer

What is a Litbuy spreadsheet?

In this guide, a Litbuy spreadsheet means a shopping comparison sheet used to organize product links, categories, sizes, listed prices and notes for further checking. Its purpose is to make research easier. An entry in a sheet does not independently verify a seller, product, service or delivery promise.

02 / The comparison framework

What belongs in your product sheet?

For a Litbuy product spreadsheet, consistent columns matter more than the number of rows. This framework helps you compare what is stated, what you have checked and what still needs an answer.

Column What to record Why it matters
Product & source Product name, category, original URL and seller identity. Helps you find the exact listing and avoid confusing similar items.
Variant & sizing Color, model, selected size and relevant measurements. A shared size label does not guarantee the same fit.
Materials & details Stated composition, construction details and information gaps. Keeps listing claims separate from assumptions based on appearance.
Cost breakdown Item price, currency, quoted shipping and applicable charges. Supports a more useful comparison than item price alone.
Photos & evidence Listing photos, available detail images and their source. Makes discrepancies easier to review without treating photos as proof of authenticity.
Status & last checked Researching, shortlisted or rejected, with a verification date. Shows which entries need another check before a decision.

This is a suggested column structure. It is not a live inventory table, a price quotation or an included spreadsheet download.

04 / Before you decide

A better shortlist
has fewer unknowns.

Keep observations precise. “Listed as cotton” is different from a verified composition, just as a displayed price is different from a complete delivered cost.

Use your notes to identify the next question to ask before you commit.

  • Confirm the exact product, color and size variant.
  • Check measurements against an item you already own.
  • Record material claims and any missing specifications.
  • Review seller identity, provenance and return conditions.
  • Request current shipping and service fee information.
  • Recheck the original listing immediately before ordering.
05 / Clear answers

Litbuy spreadsheet FAQ

Direct answers about this guide, comparison sheets and the checks worth making before a purchase.

What is a Litbuy spreadsheet?

In this guide, a Litbuy spreadsheet means a shopping comparison sheet used to organize product links, categories, sizes, listed prices and notes for further checking. A spreadsheet is a research tool; its contents do not independently verify a seller, product or service.

Is this an official Litbuy website?

No. This is an independent informational page directing visitors to CAFANS. It does not represent Litbuy or the brands mentioned, and it does not claim that CAFANS links support any particular agent or checkout service.

Does this page provide a downloadable spreadsheet?

No downloadable spreadsheet is included. This page provides category links and a comparison framework that you can copy into your own spreadsheet.

How should I compare shoes and clothing?

For shoes, check the exact model, size chart, foot measurements and listing photos. For clothing, compare garment measurements, fabric composition and fit notes. Use the current product listing rather than a category label or spreadsheet summary to confirm details.

Does a product photo prove authenticity?

No. A product photo, logo or spreadsheet entry does not establish authenticity. Verify the seller, provenance, authorization where relevant and return terms. The brand-inspired drawings on this page are editorial illustrations, not photographs of items offered for sale.

How do I estimate the total shopping cost?

Record the item price separately from domestic shipping, service charges, international shipping, currency conversion costs and applicable taxes or duties. Get current quotes from the relevant provider; this page does not supply a delivery or fee estimate.

Where do the category links go?

The category links open the corresponding CAFANS category pages. General browsing buttons open https://www.cafans.com.
